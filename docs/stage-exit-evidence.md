## Caves II descent (#413)

The results below belong to PR731's fresh capture on `ad1a60b5` (main
`0cd8427e` merged). The later docs commit and merge of DST-only main
`87ddf064` retain that shipped artifact; they add no new gameplay capture.
Advancing main does not inherit this qualification. Caves II's exit remains
refused because stage 5 has no admitted generator; PR732's Depths catalogue
import does not change that boundary.

`EV9BP18Q-caves2` carries `EV9BP18Q-descent` one floor further. Each game also clears Caves I's route 5 → 6 → 7 → 9 → 10 → 11 → 12 → 13 → 0 and Mega Fatty with its own keys. The shared tail then re-anchors in Mega Fatty's room and is graded through the trapdoor, the stage transition and the Caves II arrival. The suite is `stage-descent`, in the `@stage-long` group.

**The original's segment** is fixed fixture keys, 47,263 frames from Basement I's boss room to Caves I's corner. A research controller found the Caves I keys room by room, keeping the seeded variant that ended each room with the most health. A fresh original replays them exactly and leaves Caves I at 3/6 red with 1 coin.

**The port's segment** is recorded closed-loop per build, one leg per floor. Every leg runs the recorder's `--dodge --rewind` (`fightFlags`), and Caves I's leg has a 2,400-frame budget per route room. Those flags shape only the port's own keys.

**No seed pins.** #754 changed the port's random stream at route frame 133, so every closed-loop fight after it plays differently (Caves I descent, above). #759 kept stage-exit green with a Basement II `--dodge` pin, but no Caves I seed pin it searched reached Mega Fatty: boss salts 1–7 and 12 and route salts 1–6 each died or stalled in a route room. With `--rewind` on every leg, no leg depends on one lucky path.

**Generation inputs.** Caves II's generation reads the departing player's health and coins:
- full red health admits the Challenge and Sacrifice rooms;
- a half heart without soul hearts admits the bedrooms;
- more than four coins admits an Arcade.

The comparator grades those three predicates at the re-anchor; raw health and coins stay diagnostics. Both games leave Caves I neither full, nor on a half heart, nor holding five coins.

**Why `--rewind`.** The bot plays on through every hit, and Caves I's route rooms and Mega Fatty hit it often:
- Squirt lunges and the Dips a Squirt bursts into;
- Crazy Long Legs pinning it to a wall;
- fireplace contact while Skinnies chase it;
- Mega Fatty's landing creep and puke volleys.

Without `--rewind`, a leg survives only on a lucky path. After #754, no seed pin #759 searched found one: every pin died or stalled in a Caves I route room.

`--rewind` takes the leg back over each hit it can avoid (tools/e2e/README.md, Route recorder). Mega Fatty's red creep hurts for 125 updates after it lands, so the corner walk after the clear waits it out; a fight that leaves no creep waits no frame.
